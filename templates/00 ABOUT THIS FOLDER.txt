This folder stores the current guest-detail export and its archive.

The workbook updater does not use these files to change the score-trends workbook.
After a successful live update, the email-package analyzer uses them to prepare
anonymized new guest feedback and the raw detail attachment. Missing or corrupt
detail data blocks email drafting but does not block an otherwise safe workbook update.
